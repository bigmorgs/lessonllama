# Privacy Policy

_Last updated 24 September 2026_

## In short

Lesson Llama is a planning tool for teachers. We store the plans you write and the account details needed to let you back in and bill you if you subscribe.

We do not run analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking of any kind. There is no tracking pixel, no advertising identifier, and no behavioural profiling. We do not sell or share your data.

We never access your students' personal data.

Your plans are yours. You can export them at any time, and you can delete your account and its contents.

## Who we are

Lesson Llama is operated by Craig Morgan, trading as Lesson Llama.

For anything in this policy, including requests about your own data, contact lucy@lessonllama.app.

Where the UK or EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, we are the "controller" of the personal data described below.

## What we collect

Account information. Your email address, and your display name if you provide one. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, and profile picture URL from Google — we do not receive your Google password.

Your planning content. The units, lessons, tasks, resources, reflections, timetables, classes, semesters and settings you create. Class names are whatever you type; we do not require them to identify anyone.

Billing information, if you subscribe. We store an identifier for your customer and subscription records at Stripe, your plan, its status, and its renewal date. We never see or store your card number — card details are entered on Stripe's own payment page and never reach us.

Technical information necessary to run the service, such as the IP address your requests come from, handled by our infrastructure providers as part of delivering and securing the service.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect student personal data. Lesson Llama plans lessons; it does not hold class lists, student names, marks or reports.

We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising networks, session recording, heat mapping, or any third-party tracking. None of that code is in the product.

We do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone for their own purposes.

## A note about free-text fields

Some fields — lesson notes, reflections, differentiation notes — let you write whatever you like. Please do not enter student names or other student personal data into them.

We do not ask for it, we do not need it, and your school will usually have its own system that is the right place for it. If you do enter it, it is stored like any other text you write, and you remain responsible for it under your school's policies.

## Why we process it, and on what legal basis

To provide the service you asked for — storing and showing your plans, signing you in, syncing across your devices. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.

To take payment and manage subscriptions, where you buy one. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you, and compliance with tax and accounting obligations.

To keep the service secure and working — preventing abuse, diagnosing faults, maintaining backups. Legal basis: our legitimate interests in operating a reliable, secure service.

To connect to Google Classroom, if and only if you choose to. Legal basis: your consent, given through Google's own permission screen, which you can withdraw at any time.

To send you service messages about your account or subscription. Legal basis: performance of our contract with you. We do not send marketing email without asking you first.

## Google Classroom, and how we use Google user data

Connecting Google Classroom is optional. Nothing in the product requires it, and everything else works without it.

If you connect it, we read the list of courses you teach so that you can set up your classes without retyping them. We request only read access.

Lesson Llama's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide and improve the features you have asked for. We do not transfer it to others except as necessary to provide those features, do not use it for advertising, and do not allow humans to read it except with your explicit permission, for security purposes, to comply with applicable law, or where the data has been aggregated and anonymised.

You can disconnect at any time, in the app or at your Google Account permissions page. Your school's Google Workspace administrator may also control whether Lesson Llama can connect at all.

## Who processes your data for us

Google Cloud / Firebase — authentication, database, hosting and server functions. Data is stored in the United States.

Stripe — payment processing and subscription billing. Stripe is the controller of your payment card data, under its own privacy policy.

These providers act on our instructions, except where they are independent controllers in their own right (Stripe for payments, Google for your Google Account itself). We do not use any other third-party processor.

## Where your data is held

Our database and server functions run in Google Cloud's us-central1 region, in the United States.

If you are in the UK or EEA, this means your personal data is transferred outside your home jurisdiction. Those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum, which our providers incorporate into their terms.

## How long we keep it

Your account and planning content: for as long as your account exists. Delete your account and it is removed.

Billing records: for as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically seven years, even after you close your account.

Administrative audit logs: two years.

A local copy of your plans is also kept in your own browser so the app works quickly and during brief connection losses. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access a copy of your personal data, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, withdraw consent, and receive it in a portable format.

Much of this you can do yourself inside the app: edit your details, export your plans, and delete your account.

For anything else, email lucy@lessonllama.app. We will respond within one month. We will not charge you, and we will not make the service worse for you because you asked.

If you are in the UK or EEA and think we have got something wrong, you can complain to your data protection authority. We would rather you told us first so we can fix it.

## Security

Access to your data is enforced at the database level: rules on our servers restrict every record to the account that owns it, rather than relying on the app to ask nicely.

Payment card details never reach our systems. Secrets are held in a managed secret store, not in our code.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data and is likely to be a risk to you, we will tell you and the relevant regulator without undue delay.

## Children

Lesson Llama is for teachers and other education professionals. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 16.

If you believe a child has created an account, tell us at lucy@lessonllama.app and we will remove it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
